## Findings

### Finding 1
**Type:** Coherence — semantic overlap (contradiction)

**Requirements:** PLAT-POWER-CHARGE-001, PLAT-MECH-CHARGEPORT-001

**Issue:** The two requirements give different charge port positions. PLAT-POWER-CHARGE-001 puts the micro-USB breakout receptacle facing backwards through an opening at the bottom of the leg's lid. PLAT-MECH-CHARGEPORT-001 puts it against the outer side wall of the left foot, with its mouth at most 1 mm inside the back face, and says the floor opening of the former port is closed. PLAT-POWER-CHARGE-001 still describes the old position.

**Status:** fixed

### Finding 12
**Type:** Coherence — precision

**Requirements:** 65 requirements, i.e. every requirement in requirements/*.md. Every FUNC-POWER-*, TECH-POWER-*, TECH-BUILD-*, PLAT-POWER-*, PLAT-MECH-*, CONF-POWER-* and PERF-POWER-* requirement, plus FUNC-MECH-JOINT-001 and TECH-HARNESS-BOARDS-001.
